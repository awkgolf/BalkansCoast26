## Kimberley Cruise — day-by-day (geology & field notes)

> [!note] Geological framing
> The cruise transits the Kimberley Craton's Proterozoic sandstone plateaus (King Leopold & Ord Ranges country), the Buccaneer Archipelago's drowned fold belt, and tidal environments shaped by ~12 m macrotides. Good candidate stops for field notes: Horizontal Falls (tidal hydraulics through a sandstone gorge), Raft Point & Jar Island (Wandjina and Gwion/Bradshaw rock art on sandstone overhangs), Mitchell Plateau (basalt-capped Proterozoic sandstone), King George River (deepest gorge in the Kimberley, King George Falls ~80m).

### Day 1 (Thu 20 Aug) — Embark Broome, cruise toward Cape Leveque
Captain's welcome cocktail party; cruise north overnight.

### Day 2 (Fri 21 Aug) — Lacepede Islands
Zodiac landing on platform-reef islands; major seabird breeding site (brown boobies, roseate terns) and green turtle rookery. Afternoon: expedition staff Kimberley introduction lecture.

### Day 3 (Sat 22 Aug) — Horizontal Falls
Morning walk, Silica Beach, Hidden Island. Cruise through Buccaneer Archipelago (800+ islands). Zodiac ride through the Horizontal Falls, Talbot Bay — driven by ~12 m tidal range through a narrow sandstone gorge.

### Day 4 (Sun 23 Aug) — Raft Point
Red Cone Creek walk, swim at Ruby Falls. Cruise through Kuri Bay (pearling). Rock art gallery at Raft Point — Wandjina figures and Gwion (Bradshaw) paintings.

### Day 5 (Mon 24 Aug) — Montgomery Reef
Doubtful Bay cruise; watch Montgomery Reef "rise" as the tide ebbs — one of the largest inshore reefs, exposing turtles, manta rays, reef sharks.

### Day 6 (Tue 25 Aug) — Prince Regent River
Prince Regent River to King Cascades (terraced waterfall over rock formations); refreshments and possible swim at Camp Creek.

### Day 7 (Wed 26 Aug) — Prince Frederick Harbour
Islands, mangroves, monsoon rainforest; cruise up Porosus Creek (saltwater crocodile territory) to aboriginal art sites and rock formations.

### Day 8 (Thu 27 Aug) — Mitchell Falls
Anchor Winyalken Bay; explore Hathaway's Hideaway sandstone caves. Optional helicopter flight over Mitchell Plateau to the four-tiered Mitchell Falls. Evening beach BBQ.

### Day 9 (Fri 28 Aug) — Jar Island / Vansittart Bay
Salt flat walk to WWII DC-3 wreck. Jar Island rock art gallery — Gwion (Bradshaw) paintings, some dated to ~20,000 years.

### Day 10 (Sat 29 Aug) — King George Falls
Tranquil Bay beach walk. King George River excursion by specialty vessel and Zodiac to the base of King George Falls (~80m, tallest in WA).

### Day 11 (Sun 30 Aug) — Darwin
Disembark ~08:30; private transfer to Adina Apartment Hotel Darwin Waterfront.
