# BalkansCoast26
Trip from Montenegro to Slovenia in June 2026 covering Karst geology.

## Croatian geology
Croatia’s coast is dominated by the Dinaric Karst: thick Mesozoic limestones and dolomites shaped by uplift, dissolution, caves, sinkholes, and dry valleys. Around Istria and Kvarner, layered carbonate platforms and flysch basins create sharp contrasts between rugged stone ridges and softer, greener terrain. Offshore islands reflect the same structural trends, with long NW-SE ridge alignments controlled by regional tectonics.

## Summary of our trip last month
Last month we tracked the Karst belt northward along the Adriatic, starting in Montenegro, crossing coastal Croatia, and finishing in Slovenia. The most memorable stops were coastal cliffs, poljes, and cave systems that showed how groundwater and carbonate rock control the landscape. We came away with a clear picture of how shared geology links the coast across borders while each segment keeps a distinct local character.
