# Kimberley Trip

Overview and planning for a trip to the Kimberley region of Western Australia.

## Proposed Itinerary
- **Broome**: Cable Beach, Gantheaume Point, Roebuck Bay.
- **Gibb River Road**: Windjana Gorge, Tunnel Creek, Bell Gorge, Manning Gorge.
- **El Questro**: Emma Gorge, Zebedee Springs, El Questro Gorge.
- **Purnululu National Park**: Bungle Bungle Range, Cathedral Gorge, Echidna Chasm.
- **Kununurra**: Lake Argyle, Ord River.

## Preparation
- **Vehicle**: 4WD required for the Gibb River Road and Purnululu.
- **Permits**: National Park passes and Aboriginal land permits.
- **Supplies**: Extra fuel, water, recovery gear, first aid kit, satellite phone/PLB.
- **Best Time to Visit**: Dry season (May to October).

## Notes
- Distances are vast, ensure plenty of travel time between destinations.
- Book campsites well in advance.
